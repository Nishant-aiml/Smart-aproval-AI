1. Introduction
Smart Approval AI is an autonomous, multi-modal cognitive auditing and compliance verification platform engineered for Indian engineering institutions. It replaces episodic, 45-day paper-heavy accreditation scrutinies with real-time, deterministic verification across AICTE, NBA Tier-I SAR, NAAC SSR, and NIRF frameworks.
⚙️
Key Features
  • ✓ Multi-Modal Layout Ingestion: Docling OCR parsing complex tables, multi-column layouts & scanned scans.
  • ✓ Zero-Hallucination Extraction: Gemini 2.5 Flash strictly bounded by Pydantic v2 schemas for verified entity parsing.
  • ✓ Deterministic AICTE Verification: Automated check of 1:2:6 Cadre Ratios, SFR (1:15/1:20), and area norms.
  • ✓ NBA Tier-I 1000-Pt SAR Engine: Automated rubrics across all 10 criteria aligned with the Washington Accord.
  • ✓ Predictive Analytics (SARIMA): Longitudinal time-series forecasting detecting anomalies 12-24 months early.
  • ✓ Tamper-Proof Dossier Synthesis: One-click generation of PDF/Excel audit reports with SHA-256 digital seals.

Problem Statement
Higher education accreditation in India requires navigating multi-tiered, opaque mandates across AICTE, NBA, NAAC, and NIRF. Manual scrutiny of 1,000+ page institutional portfolios takes 45 person-days, introduces cognitive bias, causes mathematical discrepancies in SFR and cadre ratios, and results in catastrophic eleventh-hour approval rejections.
🎯
Project Objectives
1
Autonomous Layout Parsing: Extract tables, stamps, and nested hierarchies from scanned PDFs with 98%+ cell fidelity.
2
Deterministic Compliance: Eliminate LLM arithmetic errors via programmatic AICTE, NBA, and NAAC verification.
3
Outcome-Based Evaluation: Real-time 1000-point SAR rubric computation tied to Washington Accord CO-PO metrics.
4
Predictive Forecasting: SARIMA time-series alerting leadership to faculty attrition and deficits 2 years in advance.
5
Port-Isolated Architecture: Single-tunnel reverse-proxy preventing port collisions across concurrent web servers.
